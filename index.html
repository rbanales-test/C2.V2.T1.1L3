Listening to: Fundamental (440 Hz pure sine tone).

Selected: Fundamental

The lowest harmonic, and it is usually the pitch you hear. Analogy: a staircase ground floor. Most instruments build their sound on this staircase.

In short: All harmonics are partials, and harmonics above the fundamental are overtones. But not every partial or overtone is a harmonic.
